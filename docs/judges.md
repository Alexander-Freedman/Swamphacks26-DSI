# Judges + sponsors (XII)

Nothing is picked. There are 50 judges on the XII Devpost page (affiliations only, no titles): 36 industry/outside, 14 UF.
- **Sperry Tech:** 4 (their own challenge).
- **2 each:** NextEra, Roblox, Goldman, Apple, Oracle, Walmart.
- **1 each:** Vobile, Lockheed, Meta, ADM, o9, McKesson, Microsoft, SiriusXM, SLB, Labcorp, Blackhawk, Vispero, RouteSmart/FedEx, Insight Global, Everforth ECS, Brevian AI, Loandock, Avaro Group, City of Dallas, MLH.
- **UF:** DSI ×3, CpE/ECE, CISE ×2, Engineering Education, 2 other faculty, CTSI, College of Medicine, UFIT Data Platform & Analytics, C3 (career center).

**Takeaways:**
- **The "UF Data Science & Informatics" judges are DSI, the student org**, not a department. They'll want a real model and an honest eval.
- **Most industry judges are energy and field-ops:** NextEra ×2, SLB, RouteSmart, City of Dallas, Lockheed, ADM. That favors the hazard radar.
- **The UF teaching and data-science judges** favor the Mental Models Tool. One Engineering Education judge researches LLM tools for student help.
- **We don't pick who visits our table.** Keep two openers ready, a field-ops one and a learning-science one, and choose by badge. Always show one model plus one held-out eval number.
- **Vobile sponsors Best Overall**, but it's judged by the general pool. The one unforced Vobile-flavored angle is for the hazard radar: perceptual-hash dedupe of crew hazard photos (~1-2h, one line in the pitch).

## 3. How the judge mix should shape the pitch [inference throughout]

**Overall read.** About 2/3 of the general pool is industry engineers from data-heavy enterprises. Energy and field operations alone account for NextEra x2, SLB, RouteSmart, City of Dallas, ADM and Lockheed. Then there's retail and supply chain (Walmart x2, o9, McKesson), finance (Goldman x2, Blackhawk, Loandock), and big tech (Apple x2, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle x2). About 1/3 is UF, with a strong data-science and teaching flavor: DSI x3, ECE, CISE, Engineering Education, CTSI, College of Medicine. Shared theme across almost every org: **AI in production, agents, data quality, and measurable gains.** Both candidates should open with the user and the outcome, then show **one model plus one eval number**, and be ready to explain every design choice (Technical Understanding is criterion 1).

**Which candidate lands where**

| Group | A: hazard radar | B: predict-then-simulate physics | One sentence to add for this group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sperry Tech (4, own challenge) | **Strong.** It is their brief | n/a | "Every one of the six brief items is live on real EIA plants, and here is the replay of a real past storm day." |
| NextEra x2, SLB, RouteSmart, City of Dallas | **Strong.** Crews, storms, field ops | Weak | "This is the crew-side twin of what utilities are doing with weather-aware crew scheduling: it turns a forecast into a go/no-go per site." |
| Lockheed | **A** (safety-critical) | Neutral | "The stop-work rule is deterministic and tested; the ML index only ranks, it never overrides the 10-mile lightning rule." |
| ADM, o9, Walmart x2, McKesson | **A** (ops/planning data) | Neutral | "We trained on X years of NOAA storm events and checked it on a later year it never saw, with precision at the top 10 sites vs a naive baseline." |

| Apple x2, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle, SiriusXM, Roblox x2 | Even | **B** (consumer UX, interactive, creative) | B: "You commit to a prediction before the sim runs, which is the moment the misconception shows up." A: "It's built for a phone in a gloved hand in the sun: big targets, one screen." |
| Vispero | Even | Even | "Fully keyboard- and screen-reader navigable; alerts are also spoken and vibrate." (Only say it if it's true.) |

**Net.**
- **A** fits more industry judges *and* has its own 4-judge challenge.
- **B** fits the UF teaching and data-science judges and the consumer-tech judges.
- Which judges reach our table at the expo is not something we control. So whichever we build, keep two openers ready, a "field ops" one and a "learning science" one, and pick based on the badge in front of us.

## 4. Vobile (Best Overall sponsor)

- **What it does.**
  - It calls itself the "global leader in digital content protection and transaction services for the world's largest entertainment companies, platforms, sports leagues, and publishers". Its products are VideoTracker (protection across video sites), RightsID (claiming and monetizing social video), ChannelID and Vobile Insights ([vobile.com](https://www.vobile.com/)).
  - The core tech is VDNA content fingerprinting and watermarking [snippet] ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/vobile)).
  - In April 2025 it acquired Pex, an audio content identification company ([Newswire](https://newswire.ca/news-releases/vobile-completes-acquisition-of-pex-862808624.html)).
  - **AI direction:** DreamMaker (Mar 2025) is an AI video-creation platform with built-in fingerprinting and watermarking and monetization through Vobile MAX ([Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2025/03/27/vobile-unveils-dreammaker-ai-powered-creative-platform-with-copyright-protection-and-monetisation/)). MAX handles "rights identification, attribution, and transactions" ([HKEX interim 2025](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/0828/2025082801448.pdf)). An H1 2026 summary claims DreamMaker produced 10M+ USD in compute revenue [snippet, unverified] ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/vobile-group-limited-3738-h1-2026_FYKEXl7Q)).
- **Gainesville.**
  - It opened an R&D and Operations Center in the Gainesville Innovation District in April 2022, aiming for "hundreds of jobs". It picked the site for its walking-distance access to UF engineering talent ([UF News](https://news.ufl.edu/2022/04/vobile-locates-in-gainesville-innovation-district-/)).
  - CEO Yangbin Wang is a UF ECE alum and 2024 Distinguished Alumnus ([UF Eng](https://www.eng.ufl.edu/news/alumni-spotlight/vobile-founder-chairman-and-ceo-yangbin-wang-honored-as-uf-distinguished-alumnus-at-spring-commencement/)).
  - Vobile co-hosted SwampHacks XI and had 3 judges there ([XI overview](https://swamphacks-xi.devpost.com/)). There's 1 Vobile judge in XII.
  - I found no current headcount.
- **Who decides Overall.** Vobile *sponsors* Best Overall, but it's judged against the general criteria by the general pool. It isn't a Vobile brief. Past Overall winners had no content-protection angle (docs/prizes.md). So building *for* Vobile is not required **[inference]**.
- **Natural (not forced) angles** **[inference]**:
  - **A: duplicate detection on crew hazard photos.** Use a perceptual hash, which is the same idea as content fingerprinting, so ten crew photos of the same downed line merge into one pin. Store a hash and timestamp per photo as tamper evidence for the incident record. It solves a real problem in the brief (Waze-style reports get spammy) and is about 1-2h of work with `imagehash`. Mention it in one line; don't make it the pitch.
  - **B: provenance on generated content.** If B ever generates explainer clips or sim variants, tag them with a visible "generated" label and source. This is weaker, and skip it unless it's already in the build.
  - **Don't** bolt on a copyright or watermark feature just for Vobile. It would hurt the Purpose and Track criteria, and one Vobile judge doesn't decide Overall.
