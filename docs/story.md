## Top 3

1. **Hunch & Ink**: the best fit overall.
   - The name is the mechanic. Hunch = the pencil guess, ink = the truth drawn over it, so a judge gets "predict, then reveal" from the name plus one glance at the screen.
   - It's tied to the Notebook Arcade look and makes an easy logo: an ink-blot ampersand, or the verdict stamp.
   - It's clear when said out loud ("hunch and ink"), and it sounds like an indie game, which helps on the Entertainment track.
   - All five domains are unregistered (hunchandink.com/.app/.tech/.space/.study), and I found no product with this exact name.
   - Weak spot: it doesn't say "busting wrong ideas". The Wrongdex can be the name of the collection screen inside the app ("your Wrongdex").
2. **Wrongdex**: the most game-like and the funniest. It sells the collect-and-bust loop, which is our differentiator against Brilliant/Socratic tutors. Every domain is free, including .com.
   - Weak spots: it hides the predict-first moment, and the "-dex" borrows from Pokémon. It's a better feature name than product name.
3. **Pencil vs Ink**: the plainest description, also with every domain free. It has a nice "you vs reality" contest feel.
   - Weak spots: it's longer, sounds like an art tool, and loses the misconception angle.

Suggested combo: product **Hunch & Ink**, collection screen **Wrongdex**, verdict stamps **Called it / Close / Way off**.
