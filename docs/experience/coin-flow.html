C6 Mistake named

Coin streak6/7 · your mistake
Off by 3

You said 4. 10,000 real rounds averaged 7.0.

Misconception caughtin your notebook

"Random means evenly mixed."

Why it's tempting: a fair coin is 50/50, so we picture H and T taking turns. Your made-up row switched 15 times out of 19.

The right model: each flip ignores the last one, so streaks pile up. In 100 flips, a run of 5 or more shows up about 97% of the time.

Source: our simulation of 10,000 rounds; matches the known longest-run result (Schilling 1990).
